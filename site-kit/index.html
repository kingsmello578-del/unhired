The problem

You don't have a talent problem. You have a "it's all in my head" problem.

Most small agencies and freelance social media managers hit the same ceiling at 4–6 clients. Every task still goes through you, because writing down how you do it feels slower than just doing it.

So you re-explain the brand voice to every new editor. You fix the same caption mistakes. You check every post at 11pm. And hiring help just adds one more person to manage.

An SOP fixes this. It's a one-page instruction a competent freelancer can follow without asking you anything. This kit gives you twelve of them, already written for a content agency, with the AI prompts built into the steps where they save real time.

Is it for you?

Honest fit check.

This is for you if…

  • You manage social content for 2–10 clients, alone or with a small team.
  • You run a 1–15 person content or marketing agency.
  • You already use ChatGPT or Claude, but ad hoc rather than inside a process.

This is not for you if…

  • You don't have clients yet. This runs work you already have; it doesn't find clients.

Questions

FAQ

Is this a course?

No. It's a working manual: one page per job, written as instructions. Most people read the "Start here" section and one SOP on day one, then hand that SOP to someone that week.

Which AI tools does it use?

The prompts work in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or any similar assistant. Nothing is tied to one tool, and the kit includes a policy for using AI with client material safely.

Can I edit the SOPs and give them to my team?

Yes. The licence covers one agency or freelance business, including your team and contractors. You can't resell or redistribute the kit.

Do I need Notion?

No. The Deluxe databases are plain CSV files, so they import into Notion, Google Sheets, Airtable or ClickUp.

Are the numbers in the pricing worksheet real market rates?

They're worked examples that show the method. Rates vary a lot by country and skill, so the calculator is built for you to enter your own.
